# Privacy Policy for BudgetBuddy

**Effective Date:** October 3, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 3, 2026  
**Developer / Publisher:** Aguillon Designs LLC / Pilar Aguillon  
**Application:** BudgetBuddy (`com.aguillondesigns.budgetbuddy`)

---

### 1. Overview
BudgetBuddy is designed with a strict **privacy-first, offline-only** architecture. We believe your personal financial data belongs solely to you. This Privacy Policy explains how BudgetBuddy handles your information.

---

### 2. Information Collection and Storage
* **100% Local Storage:** All financial data, transactions, categories, and budget snapshots you enter into BudgetBuddy are stored locally in an encrypted/private SQLite database on your device via Android Room persistence.
* **No Account Creation:** BudgetBuddy does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number.
* **No Personal Data Collected or Transmitted:** We do **not** collect, store, transmit, process, or sell any of your financial records, transaction histories, or personal identifiable information (PII) to our servers, third parties, or advertising networks.

---

### 3. Data Export and Backup
BudgetBuddy provides features that allow you to manually export your data into a JSON file or import a previous backup:
* Exported files remain entirely under your control and are saved only to locations you explicitly select on your device or share via the Android system share sheet.
* We do not have access to any exported files or backups.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services and Analytics
* **No Third-Party Trackers:** BudgetBuddy contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or behavior tracking libraries.
* **No Advertising:** BudgetBuddy contains zero ads.

---

### 5. Permissions
BudgetBuddy requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:
* Storage/File Access (via Android Storage Access Framework) solely when you explicitly choose to export or import your data backup files.

---

### 6. Children’s Privacy
BudgetBuddy is a general-audience financial tracking utility and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date on this page.
